Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, known as the "Missile Man of India", was a great scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's space and missile development programs.

He was a simple man with big dreams who inspired millions of students to believe in themselves. His life teaches us the value of hard work, dedication, and humility.

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
